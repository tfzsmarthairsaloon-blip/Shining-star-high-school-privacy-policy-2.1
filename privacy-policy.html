Privacy Policy

Shining Stars High School App · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Shining Stars High School ("we", "us") provides this app so students can view school information, such as class details. This policy explains how the app handles your privacy.

We do not collect any personal data

The app does not collect, store, use, or share any personal information. You do not need to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for or collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, photos, location, contacts, or any other personal data from students, parents, or other users.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or any other sensitive permission.

Sharing of information

Because we collect no personal data, we have nothing to sell, share, or disclose to third parties.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, it is safe for students of all ages to use.

Changes to this policy

If the app's practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact Shining Stars High School through the school office.
